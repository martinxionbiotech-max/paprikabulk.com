# Paprika Grade Comparison Chart

A side-by-side comparison of the four Dinweys paprika powder grades.

| **Moisture** | ≤10% | ≤10% | ≤10% | ≤10% |
| **Particle Size** | 40 mesh | 40 mesh | 40 mesh | Custom |
| **SHU (Pungency)** | ≤500 | ≤1,000 | ≤2,000 | ≤500 |
| **Heavy Metals** | EU MRL | EU MRL | China GB | EU MRL |

| Your Need | Recommended Grade |
|-----------|------------------|
| Maximum color intensity | Special ASTA200+ |
| Premium food manufacturing | Premium |
| Balanced cost and quality | Superior |
| Large volume, standard color | Standard |
| Oleoresin extraction | Special ASTA200+ |
