# Specification

## Definition

A specification (spec) is a documented set of technical requirements that a paprika product must meet, covering physical, chemical, and microbiological parameters.

## Overview

Specifications are the foundation of quality assurance in paprika trade. They define exactly what the buyer will receive and provide the standard against which each shipment is tested. Every COA compares test results to the agreed specifications.

## Technical Explanation

**Typical Paprika Specification Parameters:**

| Parameter | Example | Test Method |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| ASTA Color Value | 160 min | Spectrophotometry |
| Moisture | ≤10% | LOD at 105°C |
| Particle Size | 95% through 40 mesh | Sieve analysis |
| Pungency (SHU) | ≤500 | HPLC |
| Extraneous Matter | ≤0.5% | Visual inspection |
| Total Ash | ≤8% | Furnace ashing |
| Acid-Insoluble Ash | ≤1% | Acid treatment |
| Salmonella | Negative/25g | ISO 6579 |
| Yeast & Mold | ≤1,000 CFU/g | Plate count |
| E. coli | Negative | ISO 16649 |

**Specification Types:**
- **Product Spec:** Defines the product for a specific grade or SKU
- **Purchase Spec:** Buyer's requirements for a specific order
- **Regulatory Spec:** Minimum requirements for import compliance

## Importance in Paprika Documentation

Specifications are the key reference document for procurement, QC, and compliance. Every stakeholder in the supply chain — buyer, seller, inspector, regulator — uses the specification as the standard.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Who sets the specification — buyer or seller?**
A: Both. The seller publishes standard specifications for their grades. The buyer may request modifications for specific applications. The final agreed specification is documented in the sales contract.

**Q: What happens if a shipment doesn't meet specification?**
A: The buyer has the right to reject, request a price reduction, or negotiate corrective actions. This is why pre-shipment inspection by a third party is common practice.
