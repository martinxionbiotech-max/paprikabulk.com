# Container Loading

## Definition

Container loading (container stuffing) is the process of packing packaged paprika products into shipping containers for international transport, following specific procedures to ensure cargo security and quality preservation.

## Overview

Proper container loading is essential for preventing physical damage, moisture damage, and contamination during ocean transit. A well-executed loading plan maximizes container utilization while protecting product quality.

## Technical Explanation

**Container Types:**

| Container Type | Internal Dimensions | Typical Load |
|---------------|-------------------|--------------|
| 20 ft Dry | 5.9 × 2.35 × 2.39 m | 20–22 metric tons |
| 40 ft Dry | 12.0 × 2.35 × 2.39 m | 25–27 metric tons |
| 40 ft HC | 12.0 × 2.35 × 2.69 m | 26–28 metric tons |

**Loading Best Practices:**

| Practice | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Palletization | Uniform handling, damage prevention |
| Stretch Wrapping | Unitize pallets, moisture barrier |
| Desiccant Placement | Condensation control |
| Gap Filling | Prevent shifting during transit |
| Container Lining | Hygiene protection (food-grade liner) |
| Weighing | Verify against B/L, avoid overweight |

**Inspection Points:**
- Container cleanliness and odor
- Floor condition (no nails, splinters)
- Ventilation settings (for moisture management)
- Door seal integrity
- Temperature records (if reefers used)

## Importance in Paprika Documentation

Container loading inspection records should be documented and available upon request. Proper loading affects in-transit quality preservation and arrival condition.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can condensation damage paprika during shipping?**
A: Yes. Temperature changes during ocean transit cause condensation inside containers. Moisture-protective packaging and desiccants are essential for long-distance shipments.

**Q: What is the standard pallet configuration for paprika bags?**
A: Typical configuration is 20–25 kg bags, 40–50 bags per pallet, stacked 8–10 high, stretch-wrapped. Each container holds 18–22 pallets (40 ft container).
