# Supplier Qualification

## Definition

Supplier qualification is the systematic process of evaluating and approving a paprika supplier based on quality systems, production capability, compliance, financial stability, and past performance.

## Overview

Supplier qualification is a critical procurement function. It ensures that suppliers consistently deliver products that meet specifications, comply with regulations, and operate ethically. A robust qualification program reduces supply chain risk.

## Technical Explanation

**Qualification Stages:**

| Stage | Activities | Outcome |
|-------|------------|---------|
| 1. Pre-Qualification | Document review, certifications, references | Initial approval |
| 2. On-Site Audit | Facility inspection, GMP assessment, QC lab review | Verified capability |
| 3. Sample Evaluation | Product testing against specifications | Quality verification |
| 4. Trial Order | Small-scale commercial order | Operational validation |
| 5. Approved Supplier | Full approval for regular orders | Supplier listed |

**Evaluation Criteria:**

| Parameter | Weight | Assessment Method |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|
| Quality System | 25% | Audit score, certifications |
| Product Quality | 25% | Sample testing, COA review |
| Price Competitiveness | 15% | Market comparison |
| Delivery Performance | 15% | On-time delivery rate |
| Communication | 10% | Response time, clarity |
| Compliance | 10% | Regulatory history, audits |

## Importance in Paprika Documentation

Supplier qualification documents (audit reports, evaluation scores) are part of the buyer's quality system. They should be maintained for the duration of the supplier relationship and updated periodically.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How often should supplier qualification be renewed?**
A: Annual re-qualification is standard. More frequent if quality issues arise or if the supplier undergoes significant changes (new facility, new ownership).

**Q: Can I qualify Dinweys as a supplier?**
A: Yes. Dinweys welcomes supplier qualification audits from potential buyers. Contact our quality team to arrange an audit or request our qualification documentation package.
