# Incoterms

## Definition

Incoterms (International Commercial Terms) are standardized trade terms published by the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC) that define the responsibilities of sellers and buyers for delivery, insurance, customs clearance, and risk transfer.

## Overview

Incoterms are essential in international paprika trade. They determine who pays for shipping, insurance, customs clearance, and where risk transfers from seller to buyer. Choosing the right incoterm affects pricing and logistics responsibilities.

## Technical Explanation

**Common Incoterms for Paprika Trade:**

| Term | Meaning | Seller Responsibility | Buyer Responsibility | Risk Transfer |
|------|---------|----------------------|---------------------|---------------|
| **EXW** | Ex Works | Make goods available at factory | All transportation, export/import customs | At factory |
| **FOB** | Free On Board | Deliver goods to port, load on vessel | Ocean freight, insurance, import | On vessel |
| **CIF** | Cost Insurance Freight | Freight + insurance to destination port | Import customs, destination handling | On vessel |
| **CFR/CNF** | Cost and Freight | Freight to destination port (no insurance) | Insurance, import customs | On vessel |
| **DAP** | Delivered at Place | Transport to buyer's named location | Import customs, unloading | At destination |

**Recommended Terms for Paprika:**
- **FOB (Qingdao/Shanghai):** Most common for Chinese paprika exports
- **CIF (Buyer's Port):** Convenient for buyers who want delivery to their port
- **EXW (Factory):** Suitable for buyers with own logistics network

## Importance in Paprika Documentation

Incoterms should be clearly stated in the sales contract and referenced on commercial invoices. They affect pricing, insurance requirements, and documentation responsibilities.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Which incoterm is best for first-time international buyers?**
A: CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight) is recommended for first-time buyers — the seller handles shipping and insurance, making the process simpler for the buyer.

**Q: Does FOB or CIF pricing affect product quality documentation?**
A: No. COA and product documentation are independent of incoterms. Quality documentation remains the same regardless of shipping terms.
