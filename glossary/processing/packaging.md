# Packaging

## Definition

Packaging protects paprika products from moisture, light, oxygen, and contamination during storage and transport, while providing labeling information required for trade compliance.

## Overview

Proper packaging is essential for preserving paprika quality during the supply chain. The choice of packaging depends on product form (powder, flakes, whole pods), shipment volume, destination climate, and regulatory requirements.

## Technical Explanation

**Common Packaging Formats:**

| Type | Capacity | Material | Best For |
|------|----------|----------|----------|
| PP Woven Bag | 10–25 kg | Polypropylene | Bulk powder, palletized shipping |
| Kraft Paper Bag | 10–25 kg | Multi-layer paper + PE liner | Standard powder, retail channels |
| Vacuum Bag | 5–20 kg | PA/PE laminate | Long-term storage, color preservation |
| Aseptic Bag | 200–1,000 kg | Multi-layer foil | Oleoresin, liquid extracts |
| Cardboard Drum | 25–50 kg | Fiberboard + PE liner | Premium grades, fragile products |
| Flexible Intermediate (FIBC) | 500–1,200 kg | PP woven | Super bulk shipments |

**Packaging Requirements:**
- Food-grade materials (FDA/EC compliant)
- Oxygen barrier (for color preservation)
- Moisture barrier (critical for shelf life)
- Light protection (UV-blocking for color retention)
- Tamper-evident seals

## Importance in Paprika Documentation

Packaging specifications should be documented in the sales contract and referenced on the COA. Proper packaging labeling (product name, grade, batch number, net weight, production date, expiry) is required for import customs clearance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How does packaging affect paprika shelf life?**
A: Vacuum packaging in oxygen-barrier materials can extend shelf life from 12 to 24+ months by slowing carotenoid oxidation. Standard PP bags offer minimal oxygen protection.

**Q: What is the standard export packaging for bulk paprika?**
A: 20–25 kg multi-layer kraft paper bags with PE liner, palletized and stretch-wrapped, is the most common standard for container export.
