# Safety Data Sheet (SDS)

## Definition

A Safety Data Sheet (SDS) is a document that provides safety information for handling paprika products, including hazard identification, first aid measures, handling/storage, and regulatory information.

## Overview

While paprika is a food product and generally recognized as safe (GRAS), an SDS is still required for commercial shipments to provide information about potential hazards — primarily dust inhalation (organic dust) and allergen handling.

## Technical Explanation

**SDS Sections (GHS Format):**

| Section | Typical Content for Paprika |
|---------|---------------------------|
| 1. Identification | Product name, supplier, emergency contact |
| 2. Hazards | Combustible dust (fine powder), eye irritant |
| 3. Composition | Paprika powder, 100% |
| 4. First Aid | Eye wash, fresh air for inhalation |
| 5. Firefighting | Class A extinguisher, avoid dust clouds |
| 6. Accidental Release | Vacuum, avoid creating dust |
| 7. Handling & Storage | Cool, dry, away from ignition sources |
| 8. Exposure Controls | Dust mask for airborne particles |
| 9. Physical Properties | Red powder, characteristic odor |
| 10. Stability | Stable under normal conditions |
| 11. Toxicological | GRAS, no known acute toxicity |
| 12–15. Regulatory | Not dangerous goods, not hazardous waste |
| 16. Other | Revision information |

## Importance in Paprika Documentation

An SDS is required for shipping and handling paprika products in commercial quantities. It must be available in the language of the destination country and compliant with local GHS requirements.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is paprika considered a hazardous material for shipping?**
A: Normally no. However, fine paprika powder can create combustible dust in high concentrations. Standard shipping does not require dangerous goods classification.

**Q: Do I need an SDS for paprika used in food manufacturing?**
A: Yes. An SDS is required for any chemical product in the workplace, including food ingredients, under OSHA (US), REACH (EU), and similar regulations.
