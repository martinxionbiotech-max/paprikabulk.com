# Carotenoids

## Definition

Carotenoids are a class of naturally occurring pigments responsible for the red, orange, and yellow colors of paprika and many other fruits and vegetables. The primary carotenoid in paprika is capsanthin.

## Overview

Carotenoids are the source of paprika's characteristic red color. They are fat-soluble antioxidants that accumulate in the fruit during ripening. The specific carotenoid profile — and its concentration — determines the color value (ASTA), hue, and stability of paprika products.

## Technical Explanation

**Major Paprika Carotenoids:**

| Carotenoid | Color | % of Total | Stability |
|------------|-------|-----------|-----------|
| Capsanthin | Deep red | 30–60% | Moderate |
| Capsorubin | Dark red | 5–15% | Moderate |
| Beta-Carotene | Orange | 10–20% | Low |
| Zeaxanthin | Yellow | 5–10% | Moderate |
| Cryptoxanthin | Orange-yellow | 5–10% | Low |
| Violaxanthin | Yellow | 2–5% | Low (light-sensitive) |

**Stability Factors:**
- **Light:** UV light accelerates degradation (store in dark)
- **Heat:** Above 50°C accelerates degradation
- **Oxygen:** Oxidation is the primary degradation pathway
- **pH:** Most stable at pH 4–6; unstable at high pH
- **Water activity:** Lower water activity = better stability

## Importance in Paprika Documentation

The carotenoid profile determines both the color value (ASTA) and the color shade (red vs. orange hue). High-quality paprika has a high capsanthin-to-beta-carotene ratio, producing a deep red color rather than orange.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why does paprika color fade over time?**
A: Carotenoids are susceptible to oxidative degradation. Exposure to light, heat, and oxygen accelerates this process. Proper storage (cool, dark, oxygen-free packaging) extends color stability.

**Q: Which origin produces the best carotenoid profile?**
A: Xinjiang (China) paprika typically has the highest capsanthin concentration due to intense sunlight and large diurnal temperature variation during ripening. Hungarian paprika is prized for its balanced carotenoid profile and complex aroma.
