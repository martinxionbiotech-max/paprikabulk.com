# Organic Certification

Organic certification verifies paprika is produced without synthetic pesticides, synthetic fertilizers, GMOs, or prohibited substances.

## Major Standards
- **EU Organic** — Regulation (EU) 2018/848
- **USDA Organic** — NOP (National Organic Program)
- **China Organic** — GB/T 19630
- **JAS Organic** — Japan

## Processing Restrictions
- No irradiation (steam sterilization only)
- No ethylene oxide treatment
- Segregated or thoroughly cleaned processing lines

## Documentation Required
- Current organic certificate from accredited certifier
- Scope of certification (products covered)
- Chain of custody records
