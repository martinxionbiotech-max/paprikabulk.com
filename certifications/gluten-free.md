# Gluten-Free Certification

Gluten-free certification confirms that paprika products contain no wheat, rye, barley, or cross-contamination with gluten-containing materials.

## Key Facts
- Pure paprika (100% ground capsicum) is naturally gluten-free
- Certification verifies no cross-contamination during processing
- Testing threshold: <20 ppm gluten (Codex standard)
- Shared facility processing: risk assessment required

## Certification Options
- **GFCO** (Gluten-Free Certification Organization) — most recognized
- **NSF** Gluten-Free
- **ACA** (Allergen Control Group)
