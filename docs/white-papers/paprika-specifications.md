# Paprika Specifications: A Complete Guide to Reading and Understanding Spec Sheets

## 1. Introduction

A paprika specification sheet contains all the technical information needed to evaluate a product. But the numbers only tell half the story — understanding what each parameter means, how it's tested, and why it matters is essential for informed procurement.

This guide walks through every parameter on a typical paprika spec sheet.

---

## 2. Physical Parameters

### ASTA Color Value

| Spec Line | Example | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Specification | ASTA 160 min | Product must have at least ASTA 160 |
| Test Result | ASTA 178 | This batch: ASTA 178 (passes) |

**Why It Matters:** The primary quality parameter. Determines coloring power and pricing.

### Moisture Content

| Spec Line | Example | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Specification | ≤10% | Product must not exceed 10% moisture |
| Test Result | 7.2% | This batch: 7.2% (passes) |

**Why It Matters:** High moisture promotes microbial growth, caking, and color degradation.

### Particle Size

| Spec Line | Example | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Specification | 95% through 40 mesh | 95% of particles must pass through a 40-mesh sieve |
| Test Result | 97.5% | This batch: 97.5% passes (passes) |

**Why It Matters:** Determines dispersion rate, visual appearance, and application performance.

### Bulk Density

| Spec Line | Example | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Specification | 0.4–0.6 g/mL | Packing density range |
| Test Result | 0.52 g/mL | Report value |

**Why It Matters:** Affects packaging volume, shipping costs, and mixing characteristics.

## 3. Chemical Parameters

### Pungency (SHU / Capsaicin)

| Spec Line | Example | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Specification | ≤500 SHU | Must be sweet (non-pungent) |
| Test Result | 180 SHU | Mildly pungent (passes) |

**Why It Matters:** Determines heat level. Sweet paprika buyers require low SHU.

### Total Ash

| Spec Line | Example | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Specification | ≤8% | Total mineral content limit |
| Test Result | 5.3% | Normal for paprika (passes) |

**Why It Matters:** High ash may indicate adulteration with sand or soil.

### Acid-Insoluble Ash

| Spec Line | Example | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Specification | ≤1.0% | Sand/silica content limit |
| Test Result | 0.3% | Low (passes) |

**Why It Matters:** Direct indicator of soil contamination or poor cleaning.

### Heavy Metals

| Parameter | EU Limit | US Limit | Why It Matters |
|-----------|----------|----------|----------------|
| Lead (Pb) | ≤3.0 ppm | ≤5.0 ppm | Neurotoxic, regulated in all markets |
| Cadmium (Cd) | ≤0.3 ppm | ≤0.5 ppm | Carcinogenic, EU is stricter |
| Arsenic (As) | ≤1.0 ppm | ≤5.0 ppm | Carcinogenic |
| Mercury (Hg) | ≤0.1 ppm | — | Bioaccumulative |

## 4. Microbiological Parameters

| Parameter | EU Standard | US Standard | Why It Matters |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|----------------|
| Salmonella | Negative/25g | Negative/25g | Pathogen — must be absent |
| E. coli | <10 CFU/g | <10 CFU/g | Fecal contamination indicator |
| Yeast & Mold | <1,000 CFU/g | <10,000 CFU/g | Shelf stability indicator |
| APC | <100,000 CFU/g | <100,000 CFU/g | Overall hygiene indicator |

## 5. Reading a Spec Sheet: Step-by-Step

```
Step 1: Check product name and grade
        ↓ Does it match what you ordered?
Step 2: Verify lot/batch number
        ↓ Does it match the product label?
Step 3: Check ASTA value
        ↓ Does it meet spec minimum?
Step 4: Review moisture
        ↓ Is it within acceptable range?
Step 5: Check particle size
        ↓ Suitable for your application?
Step 6: Review microbiology
        ↓ Meets destination country requirements?
Step 7: Verify certification status
        ↓ Organic, Kosher, Halal — current and valid?
```

## 6. Red Flags on Spec Sheets

| Red Flag | What It Could Mean |
|----------|-------------------|
| No batch number | Poor traceability |
| Missing microbiology data | Incomplete testing |
| Vague specifications ("typical") | No commitment to quality |
| No expiry or revision date | Outdated document |
| Different grade names confusing | Mislabeling possible |
| ASTA suspiciously high for price | Possible adulteration |

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the most important specification to check?**
A: ASTA color value — it's the primary quality and pricing parameter. Second: microbiological results for your destination market.

**Q: How much variation is normal between batches?**
A: ASTA ±10%, moisture ±1%, particle size ±3% is typical for consistent production.

**Q: Should I use a third-party lab for verification?**
A: For critical purchases, independent third-party testing is recommended. Many buyers split samples with suppliers as a quality assurance practice.
