# ISO Certification

## Definition

ISO (International Organization for Standardization) certifications are internationally recognized management system standards that demonstrate a company's commitment to quality (ISO 9001), food safety (ISO 22000), or environmental management (ISO 14001).

## Key Standards for Paprika Industry

| Standard | Title | Focus |
|----------|-------|-------|
| ISO 9001:2015 | Quality Management Systems | Process consistency, customer satisfaction |
| ISO 22000:2018 | Food Safety Management | Farm-to-fork food safety |
| ISO 14001:2015 | Environmental Management | Environmental impact reduction |
| ISO 45001:2018 | Occupational Health & Safety | Worker safety |

## Importance

ISO certification provides independent verification that a supplier operates to internationally recognized standards. It is often a prerequisite for supplying major food manufacturers and retailers.
