# Kosher Certification

Kosher certification confirms that paprika products meet Jewish dietary law (kashrut) requirements.

## Key Requirements
- Equipment must be kosher-certified or properly kosherized
- No non-kosher additives or processing aids
- Rabbi or kosher supervisor oversight
- Renewal required annually

## Major Certifying Agencies
- **OU** (Orthodox Union) — most widely recognized globally
- **Kof-K**
- **Star-K**
- **OK Kosher**
