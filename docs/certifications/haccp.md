# HACCP (Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point)

## Definition

HACCP is a systematic preventive approach to food safety that addresses physical, chemical, and biological hazards through analysis and control of raw materials, production processes, and distribution.

## 7 Principles

1. Conduct a hazard analysis
2. Identify Critical Control Points (CCPs)
3. Establish critical limits for each CCP
4. Establish monitoring procedures
5. Establish corrective actions
6. Establish verification procedures
7. Establish record-keeping procedures

## In Paprika Processing

| CCP | Hazard | Critical Limit | Monitoring |
|-----|--------|---------------|------------|
| Raw material receiving | Salmonella, pesticides | Supplier COA verification | Every shipment |
| Metal detection | Metal fragments | ≥1.0 mm ferrous = reject | Continuous |
| Moisture control | Mold growth | ≤10% moisture | Every production batch |
