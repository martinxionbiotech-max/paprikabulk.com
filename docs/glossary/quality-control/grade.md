# Paprika Grade

## Definition

Paprika grade is a commercial classification system that categorizes paprika products based on quality parameters including ASTA color value, particle size, moisture content, and appearance.

## Overview

Paprika grading enables buyers to select products that match their specific application requirements. Grades are not standardized globally — each supplier defines their grade specifications. Dinweys (Qingdao) defines four grades: Premium, Superior, Standard, and Special.

## Technical Explanation

**Dinweys Grade Classification:**

| Grade | ASTA Range | Particle Size | Typical Use |
|-------|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| Premium | 160–200 | 40–60 mesh | Premium sausages, high-color applications |
| Superior | 120–160 | 40–60 mesh | Quality seasoning, meat processing |
| Standard | 80–120 | 40–60 mesh | General food manufacturing |
| Special (ASTA200+) | 200+ | Various | Maximum color, oleoresin extraction |

**Grade Determination Factors:**
- ASTA color value (primary parameter)
- Particle size distribution
- Moisture content
- Appearance (color uniformity, absence of defects)
- Pungency level (SHU)
- Microbiological quality

## Importance in Paprika Documentation

Every specification sheet, COA, and product label must clearly state the grade. Grade determines pricing, application suitability, and customer expectations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I request a custom grade between Standard and Superior?**
A: Yes. Dinweys offers OEM specification development to meet specific ASTA, particle size, and purity requirements.

**Q: Is "Premium" the same across all suppliers?**
A: No. Grade names are not standardized. Always verify the actual ASTA value and specifications behind the grade name.
