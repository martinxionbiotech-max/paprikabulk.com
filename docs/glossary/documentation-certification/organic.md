# Organic Certification

## Definition

Organic certification verifies that paprika products are produced without synthetic pesticides, fertilizers, or GMOs, following approved organic farming practices and processing standards.

## Overview

Organic paprika commands premium pricing in developed markets (EU, US, Japan). Certification requires annual audits, documented traceability, and compliance with specific production standards from planting through processing.

## Technical Explanation

**Major Organic Standards:**

| Standard | Market | Key Requirements |
|----------|--------|-----------------|
| EU Organic | European Union | No synthetic pesticides, 2-year conversion period |
| USDA Organic | United States | NOP compliant, no prohibited substances |
| China Organic | China | GB/T 19630 standard |
| JAS Organic | Japan | Japanese Agricultural Standard |
| COR (Canada) | Canada | Canada Organic Regime |

**Organic Paprika Processing Requirements:**
- No irradiation for sterilization (steam only)
- No ethylene oxide treatment
- No synthetic anti-caking agents
- Organic-compatible cleaning agents
- Segregated processing lines or thorough cleaning between conventional and organic
- Full traceability from field to finished product

## Importance in Paprika Documentation

Organic certified products require specific labeling, certification documents, and traceability records. Documentation must include the certification number, certifying body, and scope of certification.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is organic paprika better quality than conventional?**
A: Quality parameters (ASTA, moisture, particle size) are independent of organic certification. Organic certification addresses farming practices, not intrinsic quality. Some buyers prefer organic for regulatory or marketing reasons.

**Q: Can small farms produce organic paprika for export?**
A: Yes, but they must be certified by an accredited certifying body. Group certification (internal control system) is available for smallholder cooperatives.
