# Origin Traceability

## Definition

Origin traceability is the ability to trace paprika products back to their specific growing region, farm, or cooperative, documenting the complete chain from field to factory.

## Overview

Origin traceability is increasingly important in the global spice trade. Buyers seek transparency about where their paprika comes from — not just for quality assurance, but for regulatory compliance, sustainability reporting, and brand storytelling.

## Technical Explanation

**Traceability Levels:**

| Level | Detail | Documentation Required |
|-------|--------|----------------------|
| Country | e.g., "China" | Supplier declaration |
| Region | e.g., "Xinjiang, Bayingolin Prefecture" | Regional certification |
| Cooperative | e.g., "Bayingolin Red Pepper Cooperative" | Membership records |
| Farm | e.g., "Farm #A-2026-078" | GPS coordinates, grower ID |
| Field | e.g., "Field 3B, 40°N 86°E" | Field maps, soil records |

**Key Growing Regions for Paprika:**

| Region | Country | Characteristics |
|--------|---------|-----------------|
| Xinjiang | China | Highest ASTA (160–220), mild pungency |
| Gansu | China | Balanced color and aroma |
| Inner Mongolia | China | Good yield, consistent quality |
| Shandong | China | Coastal climate, mild flavor |
| Kalocsa | Hungary | Complex aroma, 0 SHU |
| Szeged | Hungary | Premium sweet, traditional |
| Extremadura | Spain | Deep red,smoky (pimentón) |
| California | USA | Bell pepper type, mild |
| Serbia | Serbia | Balkan supply, competitive pricing |
| India | India | Cost-effective, variable quality |

## Importance in Paprika Documentation

Origin data should appear on the COA or certificate of origin. Different origins have distinctive profiles that affect product characteristics and pricing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is Chinese paprika of lower quality than Hungarian?**
A: No — they are different profiles for different applications. Chinese paprika excels in high ASTA color value at competitive pricing. Hungarian paprika is prized for its complex sweet aroma and zero pungency.

**Q: Can I specify the origin for my order?**
A: Yes. Dinweys offers origin-specific sourcing. Single-origin products are available, as well as blended products that combine strengths of different regions.
