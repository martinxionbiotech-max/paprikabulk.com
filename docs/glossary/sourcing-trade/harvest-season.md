# Harvest Season

## Definition

Harvest season is the annual period when paprika crops are harvested, typically September–October in the Northern Hemisphere, with timing varying by region and variety.

## Overview

Paprika is a seasonal crop with a defined harvest window. Understanding harvest seasons is critical for procurement planning — pricing, availability, and quality are all influenced by the time of year.

## Technical Explanation

**Global Harvest Calendar:**

| Region | Planting | Harvest | Availability Season |
|--------|----------|---------|-------------------|
| Xinjiang, China | April–May | September–October | October–September (year-round storage) |
| Gansu, China | April–May | September–October | October–September |
| Inner Mongolia | May–June | September–October | October–September |
| Hungary | April–May | August–September | September–August |
| Spain | March–April | August–October | September–August |
| Serbia | April–May | August–September | September–August |
| California, USA | March–April | August–October | September–August |
| India | June–July | December–January | January–December |

**Storage Life:**
- Dried whole pods: 12–24 months in proper conditions
- Ground powder: 12–18 months before significant color loss
- Frozen whole (IQF): 18–24 months

**Price Cycles:**
- Lowest: November–January (post-harvest)
- Highest: June–August (pre-harvest, limited old crop)

## Importance in Paprika Documentation

Harvest year affects product specifications — fresh crop has higher ASTA values, old crop may require blending. Documentation should note the harvest year for transparency.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When is the best time to purchase paprika for maximum freshness?**
A: November–December, immediately after the Northern Hemisphere harvest. Fresh crop has maximum ASTA values and the widest selection of grades.

**Q: How long can dried paprika be stored without significant quality loss?**
A: Under optimal conditions (cool, dark, sealed), dried whole paprika retains acceptable quality for 12–18 months. Powder degrades faster due to increased surface area.
